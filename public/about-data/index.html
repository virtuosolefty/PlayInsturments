Your practice belongs to you.

A guide to what works, what gets saved, and how to keep it safe.

Saved in this browser

Practice Deck stores your lessons, results, favourites and settings on this device, using browser storage. There is no account or cloud sync. Clearing site data or changing browsers can remove access to your progress.

Use Download progress backup on the learning home, or Progress → Backup → Export. To restore, open Progress and select Restore. You can review the backup before confirming; restoring replaces your current saved history and settings.

What you can play

  • Piano: click the on-screen keys, use computer keys, or connect a MIDI controller.
  • Guitar: use the on-screen strings, frets and strum buttons, or MIDI note input.
  • Acoustic instruments and microphone recognition are not supported.

Browser MIDI support varies. If your controller cannot connect, the on-screen instruments still work. This desktop experience works best in a current Chromium-based browser. Three-dimensional instruments require WebGL; a simpler two-dimensional view is available.

Sound that starts when you’re ready

Browsers need a user action before playing sound. Select Enable sound. If you still cannot hear anything, select No sound? to check the output and play a test note. Hearing the test is your confirmation; the output meter only shows that the app produced a signal.

The piano may request instrument samples from tonejs.github.io. If those samples are unavailable, the app can use a synthesized instrument. Your hosting provider and sample provider may receive normal web requests, including network metadata. Practice Deck does not send your practice history to an analytics service.

Sharing

A lesson link contains only the instrument and lesson identifier. Your scores, history and preferences are not included. Anyone opening the link starts their own session; playback never starts automatically.

If a lesson will not load

Use Retry lesson, check your connection, or return to the learning home and choose another piece. Export a backup before clearing browser data. If the page cannot recover, keep that backup and reload.
